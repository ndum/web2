In this course you build single page applications with Vue. You refresh the JavaScript these applications rely on, build a first single page application by hand and then learn with Vue how components, reactivity, routing and state work together. At the end you build a project of your own, with a framework of your choice.

All chapters follow one app. The association Lorraine Commons runs a library of things in the Lorraine quarter of Bern, where members borrow drills, tents or raclette ovens. Martina Hostettler from the board brings a new assignment in every part, and you extend the app step by step.

The course is under construction. Ready so far are part 0 with the [course overview](start/overview/), the [setup](start/setup/) and the [entry test](start/entry-test/), part 1 [JavaScript for SPAs](javascript/modules/), part 2 [SPA basics](spa/web-pages-and-web-apps/), part 3 [Getting started with Vue](vue/frameworks/), part 4 [Components](components/breaking-down/), part 5 [Reactivity and composables](reactivity/in-detail/), part 6 [Routing](routing/vue-router/), part 7 [State and data](state/where-state-belongs/), the [exam](exam/) page, part 8 [Architecture, quality and operations](architecture/structure-and-layers/), the [JS workshop](js/) with ten units, the eleven [optional topics](optional/overview/) and the project pages with the draft of the [project assignment](project/assignment/), the [README template](project/readme-template/) and the [project ideas](project/ideas/).
