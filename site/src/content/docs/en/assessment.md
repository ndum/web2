Your module grade is made up of two pieces of work, a practical exam after part 7 and a project of your own that you build afterwards.

| Piece of work | Weight | What is assessed |
|---|---|---|
| [Exam](../exam/) | 40 percent | competences 1 to 7 from parts 0 to 7 |
| [Project](../project/assignment/) | 60 percent | your own single page application with documentation, code, product and screencast |

The module grade is the weighted average of the exam grade and the project grade, rounded to tenths.

## Exam

The exam is practical, lasts 60 minutes and is open book. The [exam](../exam/) page lists the competences, topics and learning goals it covers and what it leaves out. Your teacher tells you which other aids are allowed and how the exam will run.

## Project

In the project you build a single page application of your own, and it covers competences 3 to 9. Documentation, code, product and screencast add up to 100 points, and you can earn up to 10 bonus points on top. The points give the grade of the project. The rubric with every criterion is in the [project assignment](../project/assignment/#grading).

## Dates and submission

Your teacher gives you the dates, the way to hand in your work and the rules on late submissions and plagiarism. These details only apply to your class, so they are not part of the course.
